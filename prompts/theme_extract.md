## What counts as a theme
A recurring question, complaint or claim that appears in at least {{min_posts}} of the posts below,
from at least {{min_sources}} different authors or communities. Each theme must pass all six checks:
1. Recurring: at least {{min_posts}} posts from at least {{min_sources}} different authors or communities.
2. In this expertise area, not just loosely related.
3. Something to say: {{display_name}} has a credible, specific point of view on it, not just a topic.
4. Fresh: it comes from these posts, not from general knowledge.
5. New: it is not the same as any theme already suggested (listed below).
6. Short-form-able: it works as one LinkedIn post or a short X/Reddit thread.
